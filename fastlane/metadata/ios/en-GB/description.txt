Relive the magic of the Eurovision Song Contest. DouzePoints recreates the scoreboard reveal point by point: jury spokespersons, the televote from the lowest score up, the qualifier announcements, and the winner.

PICK A YEAR, OR BUILD YOUR OWN CONTEST
• Lineups for every Eurovision Song Contest from 2004 to 2026, Junior Eurovision from 2016, and the 2026 Asian edition
• Semi-finals and a grand final, or your own stages: heats, quarter-finals, as many rounds as you like
• Jury and televote, televote only, jury only or combined, with your own points system
• Votes drawn from odds you can tune, or entered by hand
• The semi-final allocation draw, with pots
• Custom entries with your own flags for national selections or fictional contests

RUN THE SHOW
• Award points yourself, or let presentation mode play the whole night at your speed
• A different scoreboard theme for every year from 2004 to 2026
• Split-screen qualifier reveals, a winner moment with confetti, undo and restart
• Full voting breakdown and jury vs televote stats once the votes are in

MAKE IT YOURS
• Theme builder: colors, backgrounds, fonts, flag shapes and sounds
• Graphics studio: design share images from your results
• Share scoreboards, stats and running orders as images

SHARE WITH OTHER FANS
Signing in with Apple, Google or email is optional. It lets you save contests, themes and designs, publish them, browse and remix what other fans made, follow creators, and see the global leaderboard built from community contests.

Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Greek, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, and Ukrainian.

DouzePoints is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the European Broadcasting Union (EBU).
